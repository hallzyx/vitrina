Vitrina

Let buyers spin your craft. An artisan uploads about 12 photos of a handmade piece and gets a public storefront where buyers rotate it 360 degrees using only the artisan's real photos, and order on WhatsApp. No accounts. English and Spanish.

AWS Zero to Shipped entry. Category: Commercial Potential. Focus track: Startup.

Evaluate it (no code, no sign-up)

  • Example store: drag a product to spin it, open it for the buyer view and WhatsApp button.
  • Create flow: choose "Try with sample photos" to watch the real pipeline process a photo set live.
  • Example store as JSON and health check.

Uploading your own photos needs an invite phrase because it spends AWS resources. The phrase is in the Builder Center post of this submission. Everything else is open.

The problem

Small artisans sell through chat apps with a handful of flat photos. A buyer cannot turn a piece in their hands, so they hesitate, ask for more pictures, or leave. Vitrina gives them a store that shows the piece from every side, built from a phone photo session.

What makes it different

Fidelity to the real product. Background removal produces a mask only, so the piece's pixels are never altered. Each processed frame is compared with its original photo using Amazon Titan Multimodal Embeddings, and frames that drift are discarded. The listing, written with Amazon Nova Pro, is validated in code so it cannot invent materials, origin or measurements.

Honest notes

The example store and the sample gallery use demo photo sets (renders of CC0 3D scans, credited in the repository); the pipeline on them is the real one, and it was also tested with real phone photos. Fidelity scores are a coarse detector, not pixel-exact.
